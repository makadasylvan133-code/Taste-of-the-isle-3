Recipes and Tutorials

Here are some commonly prepared Grenadian dishes

Breakfast Dishes

Fry bakes & saltfish souse

Ingredients
  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • oil
  • 2 tbsp yeast

Saltfish Souse

Ingredients
  • 1 lb saltfish (soaked & flaked)
  • medium carrot (julienned)
  • ½ cup sweet peppers (mixed colors, sliced)
  • 1 small onion (thinly sliced)
  • 2 tbsp lime or lemon juice

Pancake, egg, and sausage

Ingredients
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 cup water or milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • 1 tbsp sugar (optional)
  • 1 tbsp oil or melted butter
Sides
  • 2–3 eggs
  • 2–4 sausages

Fishcake & coconut bakes

Ingredients

    Fishcake

  • ½ lb saltfish (boiled & flaked)
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ cup water
  • ¼ cup onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 tbsp parsley or chives

Bakes

Ingredients
  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup grated coconut (fresh or dried)
  • oil for frying

Lunches

Calaloo Soup

Ingredients
  • 6-9 cups callaloo leaves (or spinach/dasheen bush)
  • medium onion (chopped)
  • ½ cup celery (chopped)
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 6-9 cups water
  • 1 hot pepper (whole, optional)
  • ½ lb salted meat or pigtail (optional)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper of choice
  • 1 tbsp oil

Oil Down

Ingredients
  • 1 medium breadfruit (peeled & cut into wedges)
  • 1 lb salted meat or pigtail (boiled)
  • 1 lb chicken (optional)
  • 2 cups callaloo or spinach
  • 2 cups coconut milk
  • 1 cup pumpkin (diced)
  • 1 cup carrots (sliced)
  • 1 cup dumplings (optional: 1 cup flour + water + pinch of salt)
  • 1 medium onion (sliced)
  • 1 hot pepper (whole)
  • 2 sprigs thyme
  • 1 tsp turmeric or curry powder (for color)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp oil (optional, depending on coconut milk richness)

Pelau & Coleslaw

Ingredients
  • 2 lbs chicken (cut into pieces)
  • 2 cups rice
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar (for browning)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 medium onion (chopped)
  • 1 cup carrot (diced)
  • 1 cup peas of choice
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 2 cups water or broth
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika (optional)
  • 1 tsp seasoning salt
  • 1 hot pepper (whole, optional)
  • 2 sprigs thyme
  • 1 tbsp ketchup

Coleslaw

Ingredients
  • 3 cups cabbage (finely shredded)
  • 1 cup carrot (grated)
  • ½ cup mayonnaise

Dinner

Ingredients

Spaghetti & meatball

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • ½ lb ground pork (optional but recommended for tenderness)
  • ½ cup breadcrumbs
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 2–4 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Sauce

Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • Optional: 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes (adds texture)
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional, balances acidity)
  • Salt & black pepper to taste

Spaghetti

  • 1 lb spaghetti noodles
  • Water for boiling
  • Salt for water

Stew Chicken and Bread

Ingredients
Bread of Choice
  • 2½–3 lbs chicken (cut into pieces)

  • 1 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika (optional)
  • 1 tsp all purpose seasoning (optional)

  • 1 medium onion (grated)

Flour Porridge

Ingredients
  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 cups water (for mixing the flour into a paste)
  • 2 cups milk (evaporated milk or whole milk)
  • ½ cup condensed milk (or sugar to taste)
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • ½ tsp grated nutmeg
